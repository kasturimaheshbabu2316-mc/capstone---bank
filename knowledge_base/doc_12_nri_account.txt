Non-Resident Indians and Persons of Indian Origin are eligible for specialized housing and education loans under foreign exchange management regulations. Transactions must be conducted via Non-Resident External or Non-Resident Ordinary bank accounts denominated in Indian rupees. Loan repayment must originate from abroad via normal banking remittance channels or direct debits from verified NRE accounts. Borrowers must appoint a local power-of-attorney holder residing in India to facilitate physical execution of legal mortgages.
