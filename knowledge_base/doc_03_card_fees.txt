The primary Cred metal credit card carries an annual membership fee of four thousand nine hundred ninety-nine rupees plus applicable goods and services tax. The annual fee is waived for cardholders achieving annual spends exceeding three lakh rupees in the preceding anniversary year. Late payment charges are tiered from two hundred fifty rupees up to one thousand two hundred rupees depending on the total statement balance. Cash advances incur an instant finance charge of 2.5% of the withdrawn quantum.
