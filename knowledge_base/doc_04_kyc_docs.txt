Mandatory Know Your Customer verification requires an Officially Valid Document including a permanent account number card alongside an Aadhaar card or valid passport. Proof of current residential address must be established using a utility bill or bank statement dated within the previous three months. Salaried applicants must upload their last three salary slips and six months of bank statement records. Video KYC completion is compulsory for unassisted digital loan onboarding exceeding one lakh rupees.
