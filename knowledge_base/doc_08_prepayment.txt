Individual borrowers with floating-rate retail loans are exempt from all prepayment and foreclosure charges under central banking regulations. For fixed-rate personal and business facilities, prepayment is permitted only after fulfilling a minimum lock-in period of six completed monthly payments. Partial prepayments must exceed the sum of two monthly installments and incur a 2.0% administrative processing fee on the excess principal. Full early foreclosure on fixed lines requires ten days written advance notification.
