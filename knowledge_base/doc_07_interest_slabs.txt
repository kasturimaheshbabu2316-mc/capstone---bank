Annualized lending interest rates are tiered dynamically across three risk-based credit score brackets. Premium applicants with credit scores above 800 qualify for prime annual interest rates between 8.5% and 11.5%. Standard borrowers with credit scores between 750 and 799 receive rates between 12.0% and 16.5%. Unsecured personal loans for sub-prime applicants or elevated debt-to-income profiles carry interest rates up to 24.0% per annum.
